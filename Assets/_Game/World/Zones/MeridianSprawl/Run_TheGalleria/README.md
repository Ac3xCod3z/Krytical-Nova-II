# The Galleria

**Meridian Sprawl (Hegemony).** Map scene: `MeridianSprawl_Run_TheGalleria`. This folder is the place's own: its kit, this brief, and its concept art.

The full text, with the region kit and how the doc is read, is `Assets/_Game/_Docs/Design/Zone-Build-Kits.md` (in the Encyclopedia's Library); the Atlas card it builds on is `Design/World-Atlas.md`. Sizes follow `Design/16_world_scale.md`: a person is 1.8 m and trees are their real height.

## What is in this folder

- `Kit/`: the prefabs this place is built from, as variants of the Synty ones, already at world scale (Buildings 39, Props 19, Vehicles 5). What the whole region shares is in `../_RegionKit/`. Place from here, not from `Assets/Synty`.
- `Concept/Reference/` (where this place has one): the owner's finished-look mockup from the Figma World Atlas.
- `Concept/Owner/`: **your concept art goes here.** Name it `<what it shows>_<number>.png` (for example `Gate_01.png`); anything in this folder is yours and no tool writes to it.

## The brief

**Run: The Galleria** (2 rooms). Atrium (50 × 40 m, three galleries high, 18 m), then Food Court and Loading Dock (two linked halls, 40 × 30 m each). Mannequin-bots, escalators as ramps, muzak looping over the PA. Assets: Cyber City modular interior `SM_Bld_Base_*`, `SM_Bld_Stairs_*` as stopped escalators, `SM_Prop_Food_Shelf_*`, `SM_Prop_Vending_Machine_*`, tables `SM_Prop_Small_Table_*`, mannequins posed from `SM_Prop_Robot_Posed_*` (Cyber City); loading dock `SM_Bld_Door_Garage_*` (Sci-Fi Worlds), `SM_Veh_Truck_*`.
