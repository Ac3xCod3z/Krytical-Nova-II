# Ossuary Sigma

**Cessation (Null).** Map scene: `Cessation_Run_OssuarySigma`. This folder is the place's own: its kit, this brief, and its concept art.

The full text, with the region kit and how the doc is read, is `Assets/_Game/_Docs/Design/Zone-Build-Kits.md` (in the Encyclopedia's Library); the Atlas card it builds on is `Design/World-Atlas.md`. Sizes follow `Design/16_world_scale.md`: a person is 1.8 m and trees are their real height.

## What is in this folder

- `Kit/`: the prefabs this place is built from, as variants of the Synty ones, already at world scale (Lights 5, Props 12). What the whole region shares is in `../_RegionKit/`. Place from here, not from `Assets/Synty`.
- `Concept/`: renders of the built map, taken through the game's own comic look (made by **Krytical Nova ▸ World ▸ Render Concept Shots Of Open Map**): `_Vista` (the wide view), `_Layout` (top down) and one per point of interest.
- `Concept/Reference/` (where this place has one): the owner's finished-look mockup from the Figma World Atlas.
- `Concept/Owner/`: **your concept art goes here.** Name it `<what it shows>_<number>.png` (for example `Gate_01.png`); anything in this folder is yours and no tool writes to it.

## The brief

**Run: Ossuary Sigma** (2 floors). The Reliquary (a 60 × 40 m hall of bone niches in a circuit-board grid), then the Bone Choir (a round chamber 50 m across, ribs as arches). A skeletal catacomb grown into the basalt, arranged with machine precision. Assets: Dungeon `SM_Env_BonePile_*`, `SM_Env_Bone_Ribs_*`, `SM_Env_Tiles_Ornate_*`; Dark Fortress `SM_Prop_Skull_Pile_*`, `SM_Env_Spirit_Pool_*`; Sci-Fi Space `SM_Prop_Light_Panel_*` in red lines along the floor.
