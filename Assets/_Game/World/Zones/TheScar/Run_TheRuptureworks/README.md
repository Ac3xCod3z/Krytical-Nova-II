# The Ruptureworks

**The Scar (contested).** Map scene: `TheScar_Run_TheRuptureworks`. This folder is the place's own: its kit, this brief, and its concept art.

The full text, with the region kit and how the doc is read, is `Assets/_Game/_Docs/Design/Zone-Build-Kits.md` (in the Encyclopedia's Library); the Atlas card it builds on is `Design/World-Atlas.md`. Sizes follow `Design/16_world_scale.md`: a person is 1.8 m and trees are their real height.

## What is in this folder

- `Kit/`: the prefabs this place is built from, as variants of the Synty ones, already at world scale (Buildings 21, Ground 5, Props 11). What the whole region shares is in `../_RegionKit/`. Place from here, not from `Assets/Synty`.
- `Concept/Reference/` (where this place has one): the owner's finished-look mockup from the Figma World Atlas.
- `Concept/Owner/`: **your concept art goes here.** Name it `<what it shows>_<number>.png` (for example `Gate_01.png`); anything in this folder is yours and no tool writes to it.

## The brief

### Run: The Ruptureworks

**Lv 103-112, 4 floors.** Intake, Separation, the Cycle Chamber, the Rupture. The ruined extraction machinery itself, deeper than any faction Run.

| Floor | Size | Assets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intake** | 80 × 60 m hall of soul conduits | Sci-Fi Space `SM_Bld_Wall_Tube_*`, `SM_Bld_Wall_Pods_*`; Dark Fortress `SM_Env_Spirit_Spread_*` leaking across the floor. |
| **Separation** | three 30 m tanks over a gantry | Sci-Fi Worlds `SM_Bld_Scav_Refinery_Silo_*` as tanks, `SM_Bld_Bridge_*`; Dark Fortress `SM_Env_Spirit_Pool_*` inside them. |
| **The Cycle Chamber** | a 70 m ring | Sci-Fi Worlds `SM_Env_Core_*` ringed by `SM_Env_Core_Strut_*`, Dungeon Realms `SM_Env_Hell_Ring_Chains_*`. |
| **The Rupture** | a cavern with no floor, 100 m across | floating platforms `SM_Env_Floating_Rocks_*`, `SM_Env_Spirit_Ocean_*` (Dark Fortress) below, violet light everywhere. |

The Rupture's tie to the original catastrophe stays undecided (Lore Bible section 8); this dressing shows extraction machinery, not the first wound itself.
