# Krytical Nova — World Atlas

The owner's world map, copied from the Figma file **Krytical Nova — World Atlas** (https://www.figma.com/design/JhbgViYlKpJEsj8IKTLz5k) on 15 Oct 2026, so the regions, Home Cities, field zones and Runs can be read and linked beside the rest of the docs. The Figma file stays the source for the layout and the art; this file is its text. Story canon is the Lore Bible (`Lore-Bible.md`); level bands follow the Hostiles plan (`../Plans/hostiles-plan.md`).

**Nothing here is built** except the starter town (Zone01, `Central Plaza`), which stands in for a Home City today.

## How the world fits together

![World spider map: every Home City, zone and Run](art/world-spider-map.jpg)

Each faction has a Home City and four field zones in a line; the fourth zone's gate opens onto **The Scar**, where all four lands meet. Level bands follow the Hostiles plan: **1–22 · 20–45 · 45–67 · 65–90**, The Scar **88–105**, the Ruptureworks **103–112**. Every field zone has a **Run** (dungeon), growing with the band: 1 room, 2 rooms, 2 floors, 3 floors; the Ruptureworks has 4. Ronin maps ship with the expansion and are not visible at launch.

| Faction | Region | Home City | Lv 1–22 | Lv 20–45 | Lv 45–67 | Lv 65–90 (Scar gate) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hegemony | Meridian Sprawl | New Meridian | Transit Row (Run: Substation 9) | Crestfall (Run: The Galleria) | Alder County (Run: Silo 12) | Gantry Ridge (Run: The Coreworks) |
| Covenant | Elders-Reach | Aldhavn | Birchlight Vale (Run: Hearthbarrow) | Skeldfjord (Run: Wrecktide Grotto) | The Sylwood (Run: The Rootvault) | Barrowfell (Run: Howlstone Crypts) |
| Null | Cessation | Terminus | The Palefields (Run: The Idle Works) | The Scrapline (Run: The Disassembly) | The Needlegarden (Run: Ossuary Sigma) | Noct Caldera (Run: The Stirring Well) |
| Ronin (hidden) | Sunderpeak | Kagero | The Ember Terraces (Run: Steamhollow) | The Cane Sea (Run: The Listening House) | Blackfall Gorge (Run: The Lantern Gallery) | The Veilpass (Run: The Quiet Archive) |

The Scar (Lv 88–105, always-on PvP) holds the endgame Run, **The Ruptureworks** (Lv 103–112, 4 floors).

## Meridian Sprawl

![Concept art: Meridian Sprawl](art/concept-meridian-sprawl.jpg)

**Hegemony.** a New York that runs on souls, fraying outward into war-torn upstate

| Look and feel | |
| --- | --- |
| Tone | clinical, polished, confident inside the walls; outside, the suburbs are silent and the countryside is cratered. |
| Palette | Neon Cyan, Steel White, Deep Steel, Rust, Ash. Cyan + steel inside the walls. Rust and ash take over block by block the further you walk from New Meridian. |
| Synty packs | Sci-Fi City · Cyber City · Sci-Fi Worlds (corp walls, gantries) · Maps Sci-Fi Outpost (military walls, floodlights) · Generic |
| Run flavour | sterile tech gone wrong — emergency lighting, coolant fog, polite automated voices in empty halls. |

### Home City: New Meridian

*Home City · Safe.* The last gleaming arcology. Sealed walls, a monorail spine, corporate plazas and soul-energy pylons humming under glass. Nobody here looks doubtful.

**Layout**

Concentric rings around the Core Spire: inner plaza, Exchange ring, residential ring, wall with four gates. The north gate opens onto Transit Row.

**Points of interest**

- The Core Spire — Chancellor, Herald, Nova Rank
- Exchange Concourse — Broker, Kiosks, the Vault
- Marine Barracks — Instructor, Marshal
- The Pylon Garden — a park built on a live soul conduit

**Foliage and kit**

Manicured planters, glass-canopied trees, holo-signage, drones. Cyber City streets + Sci-Fi City towers.

*NEUTRAL GROUND · no Hostiles, no PvP · Callouts only*

### Z1: Transit Row

*Lv 1 – 22.* The commuter belt right outside the wall. Elevated rail toppled into the streets, dead traffic, holo-ads still flickering for products nobody buys.

**Layout**

Ring road hugging the city wall (matches the Zone01 anticlockwise route), river cutting off a north island where the portal to Crestfall sits.

**Points of interest**

- The Fallen Overpass — a rail bridge turned ramp
- Platform 9 — the Substation entrance
- Checkpoint Delta — Marine outpost, Wayfinder
- The Burning Depot — bruiser lair

**Foliage and kit**

Weeds through asphalt, dead street trees, wrecked cars and buses. Cyber City + Sci-Fi City props.

**Run: Substation 9** (1 room). A flooded metro power substation. Arc-light, dripping water, rogue maintenance drones still following their last order.

### Z2: Crestfall

*Lv 20 – 45.* The dead suburbs. Cul-de-sac grids, husk malls, dry fountains, lawns gone to meadow. It is the silence that gets you.

**Layout**

Block grid around a central mall and a reservoir; the highway interchange marks the far edge.

**Points of interest**

- Crestfall Galleria — the Run entrance
- The Water Tower — vista over the whole grid
- Oakridge Cul-de-sac — abandoned homes, Contract loot
- The Interchange — bruiser roams the overpass

**Foliage and kit**

Overgrown lawns, hedges gone wild, scattered birch and maple. Cyber City low blocks + Generic houses.

**Run: The Galleria** (2 rooms). Atrium, then Food Court and Loading Dock. Mannequin-bots, escalators as ramps, muzak looping over the PA.

### Z3: Alder County

*Lv 45 – 67.* Upstate farm belt strung with soul-pylon lines. Automated harvesters still cut fields nobody eats from; craters and burned farmsteads show where the Covenant raids landed.

**Layout**

Rolling patchwork fields split by a river; the pylon line is the leading line to Fort Alder and the Silo.

**Points of interest**

- Silo 12 — the Run entrance
- Pylon Junction 4 — lights the sky at night
- The Orchard Rows — dense Hostile ground
- Fort Alder — Hegemony forward base, Wayfinder

**Foliage and kit**

Apple orchards, cornfields, alder and maple, hay bales, barns. Fantasy Kingdom farm kit re-skinned with Sci-Fi Outpost fences.

**Run: Silo 12** (2 floors). Grain Deck, then the Munitions Cache. Harvester-bots turned sentries; the silo was never only for grain.

### Z4: Gantry Ridge

*Lv 65 – 90 · Scar Gate.* Strip-mined highlands and the war front. Extraction gantries, trench lines, wrecked APCs, floodlit military walls. The city's hunger laid bare.

**Layout**

Terraced ridge climbing by switchbacks to the fortified Scar Gate at the summit; floodlit perimeter, trench line across the middle.

**Points of interest**

- Extraction Gantry Alpha — a colossal drill
- The Trench Line — bruiser patrols it
- Crash Site — downed dropship
- The Scar Gate — fortified portal

**Foliage and kit**

Scorched pines, bare rock, snow on the upper ridge. Sci-Fi Worlds corp walls + Maps Sci-Fi Outpost military kit.

**Run: The Coreworks** (3 floors). Processing Deck, the Bore Shaft, the Core Chamber. The extraction bore itself — soul-light leaking from every seam.

## Elders-Reach

![Concept art: Elders-Reach](art/concept-eldervales.jpg)

**Covenant.** ethereal highlord vikings and elven houses in a European landscape scarred by raids

### Home City: Aldhavn

*Home City · Safe.* A hall-city at the head of a fjord. Viking longhalls on the water, elven spires on the terraces above, one pact and one wall.

**Layout**

Tiered: harbor and market at the water, the hall ring in the middle, elven terraces and the Ancestor Grove at the top.

**Points of interest**

- The Pact Hall — Chancellor, Herald
- Harbor Market — Broker, Kiosks
- The Ancestor Grove — the shrine where souls once returned
- The Rune Forge — Armorer, Fixer

**Foliage and kit**

Pines, birch, flower lines and footpaths from Elven Realm; longhalls and docks from Viking Realm.

*NEUTRAL GROUND · no Hostiles, no PvP · Callouts only*

### Z1: Birchlight Vale

*Lv 1 – 22.* Sunlit birch meadows and standing stones. Lofthollow, the first village, is still smoking from a Hegemony extraction raid.

**Layout**

A broad vale with a river down the middle and the Stone Ring at its heart; Elk Pass leads on to the coast.

**Points of interest**

- Lofthollow ruins — burned village, first Contracts
- The Stone Ring — ancient runestone circle
- The Old Mill — Wayfinder
- Elk Pass — bruiser

**Foliage and kit**

Birch, wildflower clumps, berry bushes, soft grass. Elven Realm flowers + Viking Realm pines.

**Run: Hearthbarrow** (1 room). A chieftain's barrow where the dead will not rest. One torchlit hall, one angry king.

### Z2: Skeldfjord

*Lv 20 – 45.* Sea cliffs, black-sand coves, longship wrecks. A drowned village shows at low tide. Gulls, surf and wind.

**Layout**

A coastline strip: cliff path above, tidal flats and coves below, the Sea-Stack shrine out on the point.

**Points of interest**

- The Drowned Village — walk it at low tide
- Wreck of the Sigrun — longship, bruiser
- Cliff Watchtower — Wayfinder
- Sea-Stack Shrine

**Foliage and kit**

Windswept pines, kelp, heather, driftwood. Viking Realm cliffs and boats.

**Run: Wrecktide Grotto** (2 rooms). Wreck Cavern, then the Tide Hall. A sea cave of broken longships and the warriors who went down with them.

### Z3: The Sylwood

*Lv 45 – 67.* Elven old-growth. Colossal trees, lantern-lit tree-houses, shafts of light — and scorched clearings where extraction crawlers cut through.

**Layout**

Two levels: the Canopy Road of walkways above, the fern floor below. The great tree marks the Rootvault.

**Points of interest**

- The Canopy Road — elevated walkway loop
- The Great Tree — Rootvault entrance
- The Scorched Clearing — crashed crawler, bruiser
- Moon Lantern Court — Wayfinder

**Foliage and kit**

Giant trees, ferns, glow-flowers, hanging moss and leaves. Elven Realm at full density.

**Run: The Rootvault** (2 floors). Root Hall, then the Ossuary Hollow. The elven dead sealed beneath the great tree, no longer sealed.

### Z4: Barrowfell

*Lv 65 – 90 · Scar Gate.* Snowline moors of barrow mounds and runestone circles. Wind, ice and the fells rising toward the Scar.

**Layout**

High moor of mound fields around a frozen lake; the carved arch of the Scar Gate stands at the top of the pass.

**Points of interest**

- The Runestone Circle
- The Frozen Lake — bruiser on the ice
- The Howlstone — crypt gate
- The Scar Gate — great carved arch

**Foliage and kit**

Snow pines, dead heather, icicle cliffs, snow mounds. Viking Realm winter kit.

**Run: Howlstone Crypts** (3 floors). Barrow Halls, the Hall of Kings, the Deep Cairn. A frozen crypt city under the fells.

### Home City: Terminus

*Home City · Safe.* A crater city of gear and bone. Iron fortress walls, conveyor streets, skeletal and machine citizens moving in perfect order. Nobody raises their voice.

**Layout**

A spiral road winding down the crater wall to the Assembly Core at the bottom; the Ossuary Spire rises from the rim.

**Points of interest**

- The Assembly Core — Chancellor, Herald
- The Reclamation Yards — Armorer, Fixer
- The Ossuary Spire — the dead keep their records here
- Conveyor Concourse — Broker, Kiosks

**Foliage and kit**

No plants. Cable vines, bone pillars, cold violet lamps. Dark Fortress walls + Sci-Fi Space interiors.

*NEUTRAL GROUND · no Hostiles, no PvP · Callouts only*

### Z1: The Palefields

*Lv 1 – 22.* Bone-white dust flats under a green sky. Blob growths pulse in the hollows and a half-buried colossus arm reaches out of the plain.

**Layout**

Flat open plain pocked with craters; the colossus arm is the landmark you steer by, the dead Hegemony pylon marks the far edge.

**Points of interest**

- The Colossus Arm — climbable, vista
- The Blob Fields — dense Hostile ground
- Dust Crater — bruiser
- The First Pylon — a dead extraction pylon, Wayfinder

**Foliage and kit**

Blob growths, alien spikes, crater spirals, no grass. Sci-Fi Worlds environment set.

**Run: The Idle Works** (1 room). A stalled fabrication hall where unawakened machines twitch on the line. Cold strip-light, one great press.

### Z2: The Scrapline

*Lv 20 – 45.* A trench grid of machine graveyards. Chassis stacked like strata, scrap bridges across the cuts, magnetic storms rolling over the plateau.

**Layout**

Parallel trenches cut across a plateau; scrap bridges link the plateaus, the crashed hauler blocks the central cut.

**Points of interest**

- The Chassis Wall — a cliff of stacked machines
- Scrap Bridge — chokepoint crossing
- The Magnet — crashed hauler, bruiser
- Reclamation Camp — Wayfinder

**Foliage and kit**

Cable vines, rust lichen, scattered hull plates. Sci-Fi Worlds crate buildings + Dark Fantasy grunge.

**Run: The Disassembly** (2 rooms). The Line, then the Press. Where the Hegemony once broke machines for parts; the machines remember the sequence.

### Z3: The Needlegarden

*Lv 45 – 67.* A basalt spire forest hung with ossuaries. Violet crystal light, absolute silence, bone reliquaries swaying on chains between the needles.

**Layout**

Vertical: stair-carved paths spiral up the spires, chain bridges link them. The Cathedral Spire holds the Run.

**Points of interest**

- The Hanging Ossuaries
- Crystal Grotto — violet light, bruiser
- The Cathedral Spire — Run entrance
- Alien Ruin — something older than the factions

**Foliage and kit**

Crystal clusters, fungal shelves, basalt columns. Dark Fantasy basalt + Sci-Fi Worlds alien ruins.

**Run: Ossuary Sigma** (2 floors). The Reliquary, then the Bone Choir. A skeletal catacomb grown into the basalt, arranged with machine precision.

### Z4: Noct Caldera

*Lv 65 – 90 · Scar Gate.* A glassed crater rim under an aurora of trapped souls. Tremors from something stirring beneath. The Scar Gate waits on the far rim.

**Layout**

Ring road around a central pit; the Observation Core sits on the near rim, the Scar Gate on the far one, the Well drops into the middle.

**Points of interest**

- The Glass Rim — fused earth, vista over the Scar
- The Stirring Well — Run entrance
- Observation Core — ruined corp station, bruiser
- The Scar Gate

**Foliage and kit**

Nothing grows. Fused glass, cracked edges, cloud rings. Sci-Fi Worlds crater and core pieces.

**Run: The Stirring Well** (3 floors). Bore Rim, the Descent, the Listening Chamber. Down toward whatever stirs beneath Nova — its nature stays open (Lore Bible §10).

### Home City: Kagero

*Hidden Capital · Safe.* A terrace city in a steam-warmed valley. Paper lanterns, lacquered gates, and machines, skeletons, humans and elves sharing one street. Named for heat-haze: seen and not seen.

**Layout**

Terraces climb the valley wall from the hot springs at the base to the Council at the top; one hidden road leads out to the Ember Terraces.

**Points of interest**

- The Council of Three — Chancellor, Herald
- The Steam Baths — social hub
- The Mixed Market — Broker, Kiosks
- The Shrine of the Unerased — the names they kept

**Foliage and kit**

Cherry and dark pines, moss, steam vents, lantern lines. Samurai Empire buildings + Elven Realm flowers.

*Hidden at launch. Exists through the whole base game; the expansion reveals it. Nothing before that may prove it is real.*

### Z2: The Cane Sea

*Lv 20 – 45.* A bamboo sea that whispers. Listening posts on stilts, paths that vanish when the wind moves the canes.

**Layout**

Maze-like bamboo with clearings; the dry riverbed is the one straight line through it.

**Points of interest**

- Fox Shrine — bruiser

**Foliage and kit**

Bamboo at full density, ferns, stone lanterns. Samurai pack bamboo + Elven Realm ferns.

**Run: The Listening House** (2 rooms). The Hall of Ears, then the Cellar. A half-sunken shrine where the assassins learned to listen before they learned to kill.

### Z3: Blackfall Gorge

*Lv 45 – 67.* Black-water falls, rope bridges, lantern-cut cliffs. Roar and spray everywhere; the light comes from ten thousand lanterns in the rock.

**Layout**

A gorge with cliff paths on both walls and rope bridges across; the Great Fall closes the far end.

**Points of interest**

## The Scar

### Zone: The Scar

*Lv 88 – 105 · Always-On PvP.* A vast rent of glassed earth with a severed extraction titan lying across it and an aurora of soul-light overhead. Four gates open onto it — three at launch, the Ronin descent hidden until the reveal. The field zones are already cross-faction hostile; this is where everyone is pushed into the same valley.

**Layout**

A long valley. Hegemony, Covenant and Null gates at three ends, the Ronin descent high on the north wall. Three Beacon sites on the flanks, the Titan crossing in the centre, the Ruptureworks portals under the Titan's chest. Bruisers at 98 and 105 roam the crossing.

**Points of interest**

- The Severed Titan — the broken extraction machine, central crossing
- Beacon North / East / West — Beacon War objectives
- The Glass Cliffs — sniper ridges above the crossing
- The Ruptureworks portals — endgame entry

**Foliage and kit**

Nothing. Black glass, ash drifts, cracked edges, cloud rings, aurora light. Sci-Fi Worlds core and crater pieces.

**Run: The Ruptureworks** (4 floors · Lv 103 – 112). Intake, Separation, the Cycle Chamber, the Rupture. The Hostiles Plan's Zone06 band lives here: the endgame Run every faction fights to reach first.

*Combat modes: Siege Night, Beacon War, The Scar (Lore Bible §8). Mechanics, schedules and objectives are not yet canon — this card is layout and dressing only.*

## Rules the layout follows

How these 21 zones line up with what is already built, so the atlas does not fight the Hostiles Plan or the Lore Bible.

**Layout**

Home City = neutral ground (no Hostiles, no PvP). Field zones Z1–Z4 use the Hostiles Plan bands 1–22 / 20–45 / 45–67 / 65–90, one species per Hostile Zone, levels rising with distance from the entry gate, Bruisers at the far edge. Z4's gate is the only way into the Scar. The Scar is 88–105 (Zone05, contested). The Ruptureworks carries the 103–112 band (Zone06).

**Points of interest**

- Runs grow with the band: 1 room → 2 rooms → 2 floors → 3 floors; the Ruptureworks is 4
- Each region's Runs share one flavor (banner) so a Null cave never feels like a Covenant one
- Every zone names a Wayfinder (Transit) and one Bruiser lair, matching the NPC glossary
- Naming follows the factions doc: corporate-cold / norse-elven / designations-and-endings / veil-and-ember

**Foliage and kit**

Foliage escalates by region: manicured → wild (Hegemony); meadow → forest → snow (Covenant); nothing but growths and crystal (Null); steam → bamboo → spray → ice (Ronin).

*Open for you to decide: Ronin colours and crest; whether the Scar's rent is the original extraction site; the names of the Beacon sites; hub-city district names beyond the ones listed.*
