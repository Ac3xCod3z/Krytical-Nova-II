KITSUNE SPIRIT, the first form: timings of its clips, for effects in Unity.
The same section is in Downloads/Pets/KitsuneSpirit/README.txt, beside the exported package. Source: anim_kitsune.py.

TIMINGS FOR EFFECTS (added 11 Oct 2026; frame, then seconds from the start of the clip; 24 frames a second)
  Idle        145 frames, loops (6.0 s)
    tail        sways the whole loop, one sway every 3 s
    blinks      shut on 42 (1.71 s) and 106 (4.38 s)
    ear flicks  left 21 (0.83), right 58 (2.38), left 96 (3.96), right 119 (4.92) with left 121 (5.00); 8 frames each
    breathing   one breath every 48 frames (2 s)
  Blink       12 frames: shut 3-6 (0.08-0.21 s), open again by 10
  LookAround  60 frames: turns to its left 1-14, holds to 22 (0.88 s), turns to its right 22-38, holds to 46 (1.88 s), back by 60
  PawLift     36 frames: left front paw lifts 1-10, held up 10-20 (0.38-0.79 s), set down by 30 (1.21 s)
  TailCurl    32 frames: curls up 1-10, held 10-20 (0.38-0.79 s), lets go by 28, small spring to 32
  SpinChase   52 frames: dips onto its front legs 1-8, pushes off 8-13 (front paws land 13, 0.50 s), a full turn is
              reached on 40 (1.63 s), overshoots and settles 41-52. Turns to its left. Ears lie back 9-36.
  Walk        24 frames, loops: paws land left hind 1, left front 7, right hind 13, right front 19 (footsteps)
  Run         12 frames, loops: hind paws land 1 and 2, front paws 6 and 7

EFFECT IDEAS (none of this is in the clips)
  Idle        a soft glow or motes off the tail tip now and then; a glint on each ear flick
  Blink, LookAround   nothing needed; LookAround can carry a "?" or a scan line when it looks each way (14, 38)
  PawLift     a small sparkle or paw-print mark under the lifted paw while it is held (10-20)
  TailCurl    a soft ring of light where the tail curls (10-20), let go with a puff on 28
  SpinChase   a line trail off the tail tip showing the movement: a ring drawn 11-40, brightest 13-31, fading as it
              slows; a puff of dust under the front paws on the push-off (8-13)
  Walk, Run   paw dust on the paw frames above; in Run a short trail off the tail
