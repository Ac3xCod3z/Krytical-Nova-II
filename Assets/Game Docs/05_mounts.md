# Twelve Sky 2 — Module 05: Mounts

## 1. Mount role
Mounts provide movement while also acting as a character-stat system.

## 2. Normal mount progression
Mounts can be upgraded through quality/progression systems and can carry combat-relevant abilities.

## 3. Legendary Mounts
Current Reborn documentation describes Legendary Mounts as higher-tier mounts crafted using an existing 15% mount.

Legendary Mounts:
- Are rideable and tradeable regardless of level.
- Are generated with three random ability stats.
- Can receive an additional ability through Spirit Stones.

## 4. Example Legendary Mount stat structure
Documented examples include combinations such as:
- Health + Attack + Attack Attack.
- Health + Attack + Critical.
- Health + Defense + Critical.
- Attack Attack + Critical + a special mount skill.
- Health + Critical + a special mount skill.
- Health + Critical Defense + a special mount skill.

The exact roll ranges depend on the mount.

## 5. Spirit Stone
Spirit Stones add one additional random ability from a large ability pool. Using another Spirit Stone can change that extra ability.

This creates:
Mount acquisition → Random base abilities → Extra ability roll → Re-roll/optimization.

## 6. Absorption
A mount can be absorbed temporarily. The current Reborn documentation states that absorption makes the mount visually disappear while its mount abilities remain active, and adds an additional +10 stat increase.

## 7. Special mount skills
Some legendary mounts have unique effects tied to their identity, such as survival effects or multi-target attack effects.

## 8. Design takeaway
Mounts are not simply transportation:
Mount = mobility + passive stats + randomization + special ability + optimization.
