# Twelve Sky 2 — Module 08: Factions, Wars & Territory

## 1. Faction structure
The classic game revolves around three major factions:
- Noble Dragon.
- Royal Serpent.
- Grand Tiger.

Faction identity controls:
- Starting territory.
- Faction combat skills.
- Allies/enemies.
- War participation.
- Social organization.

## 2. Alliance system
Faction leaders can establish alliances, adding a strategic layer above normal faction conflict.

## 3. War structure
Classic documentation describes scheduled, level-bracketed wars beginning from low levels and progressing through level ranges.

A typical war flow is:
Notification → Entry → Waiting/preparation → Gates open → Objective combat → Result/reward.

## 4. Annihilation
One documented war type is Annihilation.

The basic objective is elimination of opposing participating clans/teams rather than capturing a conventional point.

## 5. Holy Stone warfare
Holy Stone Battles are the major strategic faction conflict.

The central idea:
- Defend your faction's strategic stones.
- Attack the opposing faction's stones.
- Break defensive structures.
- Push into enemy territory.
- Coordinate large numbers of players.

## 6. Golden Moon Temple
A later structured battle uses two stages.

Stage 1:
- Protect Golden Moon Holy Stones from monsters.
- Three clans compete.
- The two strongest defenders advance.

Stage 2:
- The surviving clans attack each other's Holy Stones.
- Central formation control becomes important.
- Destroying all enemy stones wins.

This demonstrates a particularly useful hybrid design:
PvE defense → PvP objective assault.

## 7. Faction Leadership
Current Reborn documentation describes monthly Faction Leader elections.

The leader:
- Coordinates major faction activities.
- Leads Holy Stone warfare.
- Selects Vice Faction Leaders.

Vice Leaders:
- Help coordinate players.
- Support battlefield operations.
- Manage faction defensive infrastructure.

## 8. Faction infrastructure
Leadership can create:
- Catapults.
- Alert systems.
- Construction resources.
- Defensive towers.

## 9. Three documented tower roles
Rising Force Castle:
- Improves CP gains.

Golden Bridge Castle:
- Improves Silver gained from PvM.

Mindfulness Castle:
- Improves EXP gained from PvM.

These towers can be upgraded through multiple levels.

## 10. Strategic economy
Faction warfare therefore changes more than territory ownership.

Owning/protecting infrastructure can improve:
- EXP generation.
- Silver generation.
- Contribution generation.

Destroying enemy infrastructure can produce additional resources.

## 11. Design takeaway
The faction system turns individual progression into an MMO-scale strategy layer:

Player → Clan/Guild → Faction → Leadership → Territory → Infrastructure → War → Rewards → Stronger faction.
