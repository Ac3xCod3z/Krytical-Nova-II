# Twelve Sky 2 — Module 03: Equipment & Enhancement

> This module intentionally covers the non-weapon equipment and enhancement architecture rather than weapon-specific mechanics.

## 1. Equipment progression
Equipment is a long-term progression layer rather than disposable loot.

Major equipment slots documented by current Reborn material include:
- Armor
- Gloves
- Boots
- Ring
- Necklace
- Cape
- Additional specialized/endgame equipment

## 2. Reinforcement
Reinforcement increases an item's effectiveness through enhancement stages.

The system uses reinforcement materials and has success/failure considerations. High-end systems add protection tickets and special reinforcement materials.

## 3. Refining
Refine is a separate progression layer from basic reinforcement.

Current Reborn documentation says refining can increase stats on:
- Armor
- Gloves
- Boots
- Rings
- Necklaces
- Capes
- Weapons (not detailed here)

Refining uses Refining Stones, Purple Stones and related materials.

## 4. Combining
Combination merges compatible items to increase stats. Current WarLord documentation describes up to 12 combination stages, with a failure chance and restrictions on already-combined materials.

## 5. WarLord equipment
WarLord items exist in Rare and Epic grades.

WarLord 1 can be upgraded to WarLord 2 using a WarLord Upgrade Stone and Silver. Successful upgrades preserve the same broad grade/type/set relationship.

## 6. Requiem equipment
Requiem is a later legendary equipment tier.

Current documentation states:
- Requires Rebirth 12+.
- Six-piece configuration: armor-equivalent pieces plus ring, necklace and weapon.
- Requiem and WarLord pieces can be mixed.
- Requiem pieces have their own refining system.
- Up to five refinement results can be applied, with randomized values.
- Chaos Stones can reset refinement.

## 7. Set bonuses
Endgame equipment is not only about individual item strength. Wearing specific combinations activates additional bonuses.

## 8. Design takeaway
The equipment architecture is:
Loot → Reinforce → Combine → Upgrade tier → Refine → Assemble set bonuses → Replace/optimize.

This is one of Twelve Sky 2's strongest long-term retention loops.
