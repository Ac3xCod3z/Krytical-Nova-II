Vendor Voice Audition

Every NPC a player can talk to in Concord, with two voices each, both the gender of the NPC's model. Each voice was made once from a longer voice sample, and every one of its five lines was cloned from that sample, so all five are the same person. Line 1 is the greeting the game shows today; lines 2 to 5 are new, so a vendor can say something different each time a player walks up. For each vendor, pick a voice, untick any line you don't want, and write a better line or a note if you like. Picks save here and Claude reads them.

Same-voice check: a speaker-recognition model compared every line with its voice sample. The number beside a line is that match (1.00 would be identical; two different people of one NPC scored 0.89 to 0.94). Every line shown passed: at least 0.93, and clearly closer to its own voice than to the other one. Lines that kept failing were scrapped, not shown.
